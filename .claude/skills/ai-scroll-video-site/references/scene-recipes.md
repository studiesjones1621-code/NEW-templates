# Scene recipes by industry

Each scene: **stills** (gen_image.py) → **clip** (gen_scene.py) → one line of copy. Write prompts with camera movement, lighting and "no people, no text". Add "keep the exact same camera angle, framing and objects" to every edit prompt.

## HVAC (proven: Prime Air)
| Scene | Stills | Clip prompt (shape) | Copy |
|---|---|---|---|
| X-ray reveal | condenser beside house, golden hour → edit: unit as glowing teal holographic x-ray, night blue | first+last: slow push-in, scan line sweeps, casing dissolves into x-ray | "Comfort, checked to the coil." → "Every part, checked." + 4 part cards |
| Burners | — | text: macro, burners ignite one by one, blue flames | "Blue flame. Good sign." (yellow = poor combustion/CO risk) |
| Warm room (dial) | frosty dawn living room → edit: same room warm sunset, lamp on | first+last: static camera, frost melts, light warms | "Turn up the heat." Dial 60–74°F scrubs frames |
| Airflow | snowy colonial at blue hour | first: FPV glides to window, through glass, down to floor vent, into duct tunnel | "Ride the airflow." → "Up to 30% never makes it." (ENERGY STAR 20–30%) |
Tools: filter clean→dirty slider, furnace / heat pump / mini-split picker, $5,000 repair-or-replace rule.

## Roofing
- Storm to fresh roof (first+last: torn shingles in rain → new architectural shingles in sun): "Built for the next storm."
- Layer x-ray (roof as cutaway: decking, underlayment, ice shield, shingles): "Five layers. Zero shortcuts."
- Drone flyover of a street, settling on one roof: "We'll show you the photos before we quote."
- Tools: roof age × storm damage checklist, material picker (asphalt / metal / tile), financing estimate.

## Plumbing
- Pipe x-ray under a sink, leak glowing red: "Small drip. Big bill."
- Camera inside a drain line (sewer-camera look) to a root clog: "We look before we dig."
- Water heater: rusty tank → new tankless (first+last): "Hot water that doesn't run out."
- Tools: water-heater age slider, leak-cost calculator (gallons per day × rate).

## Electrical / solar
- Panel x-ray: breakers glowing, one overloaded: "Your panel is talking."
- Sun moving over a roof, panels fill in (first+last): "Make your own power."
- Tools: panel age/amps checker, bill → savings estimate.

## Auto repair / detailing
- Engine x-ray from a real-looking car (first+last): "We see what the dash light means."
- Paint correction: swirled hood → mirror finish (first+last), slider scrubs: "Before. After. Same car."
- Tools: service-interval slider by mileage, quote request.

## Med spa / dental (use real-practice rules: no promised results)
- Skin macro: texture → smooth (first+last), slider scrubs (show "results vary").
- Clinic fly-through from the street to the treatment chair: "Walk in like you've been here."
- Smile veneer reveal (dental): before → after.
- Tools: treatment picker, membership calculator.

## Landscaping / pools
- Yard time-lapse: bare dirt → finished patio and plants (first+last).
- Pool: green water → crystal blue (first+last), slider scrubs: "Green to swim-ready."
- Tools: yard size × service cost estimate, season planner.
